Cities I've Lived In

  • Liverpool, UK

    Liverpool is a vibrant maritime city in northwest England, famous for its rich musical heritage as the birthplace of The Beatles. The city's iconic waterfront, with its Three Graces buildings, is a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Liverpool boasts world-class museums, a passionate football culture, and a warm Scouse spirit that makes it one of the UK's most characterful cities.

  • Leeds, UK

    Leeds is a dynamic city in West Yorkshire, blending Victorian grandeur with modern urban energy. As one of the UK's largest financial centers outside London, it offers a thriving business scene alongside excellent shopping, dining, and nightlife. The city is surrounded by beautiful Yorkshire countryside and maintains strong cultural connections through its theaters, galleries, and festivals.

  • Bangalore, India

    Bangalore, officially known as Bengaluru, is India's Silicon Valley and a hub of innovation and technology. The city enjoys a pleasant climate year-round, earning it the nickname "Garden City" for its numerous parks and tree-lined streets. With its cosmopolitan culture, thriving startup ecosystem, and blend of traditional South Indian heritage with modern development, Bangalore is one of India's most dynamic metropolises.

  • Yokohama, Japan

    Yokohama is Japan's second-largest city and a major port located just south of Tokyo. The futuristic Minato Mirai waterfront district showcases stunning modern architecture, while Chinatown—the largest in Japan—offers incredible food and culture. With its fascinating blend of international influences, maritime history, and contemporary attractions, Yokohama provides a more relaxed alternative to the bustle of nearby Tokyo.

  • Tokyo, Japan

    Tokyo is an exhilarating metropolis where ancient temples exist alongside neon-lit skyscrapers and cutting-edge technology. As one of the world's most populous cities, it offers an endless array of neighborhoods to explore, from the fashion-forward streets of Harajuku to the traditional gardens of the Imperial Palace. Tokyo seamlessly blends tradition and innovation, making it one of the most captivating cities on Earth.

  • London, UK

    London is a global powerhouse and one of the world's most diverse and influential cities. With iconic landmarks like Big Ben, Tower Bridge, and Buckingham Palace, it perfectly balances its rich history with contemporary culture. The city offers world-class museums, theater, cuisine from every corner of the globe, and a unique energy that comes from being a true international crossroads.

  • Norwich, UK

    Norwich is a charming medieval city in East Anglia with a remarkable heritage of historic churches and cobbled streets. Once one of England's most important cities, it retains a strong independent spirit with thriving local businesses, markets, and a vibrant arts scene. The city offers a perfect blend of history, culture, and relaxed East Anglian living, all while being surrounded by the beautiful Norfolk countryside and coastline.
